Privacy Policy

What this site does and does not collect.

Privacy No cookies No analytics

This portfolio is a static website. It has no forms, accounts, cookies or analytics, so it does not collect personal data about you. This page explains the few things that still happen when you visit, and how to reach me with questions.

1.What this site collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no contact form, no tracking script and no advertising. This site does not set cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept or decline.

2.Third-party services

To display correctly, pages load a few resources from other providers:

  • Google Fonts, for typefaces
  • jsDelivr, for icons and animation scripts

When your browser requests these files, the provider can see your IP address and basic browser details. That is handled under each provider's own privacy policy, not by me. The resume link opens Google Drive, and the GitHub and LinkedIn buttons take you to those sites, where their own policies apply.

3.Embedded demo sites

The Mori and Viberist case studies run the real websites inside this page. Neither adds tracking here. The Viberist demo includes an enquiry form for that studio: if you submit it, your message is sent through Formspree to the studio's inbox, not to me.

4.If you email me

If you write to the address below, I use your email address and message only to reply to you. I do not sell or share them, and I keep them only as long as the conversation needs. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

5.Questions and changes
