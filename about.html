More about me

I'm a bit of a cliché, in the best way. I'm into a lot of the same things as most people, which helps me connect easily with others. I'm naturally tuned in to what people are thinking or feeling, even without a conversation. That awareness helps me read the room, spot patterns, and design experiences that make sense to real users.

Outside of design, I'm into sports like badminton and football, and I'm always up for trying something new, like wall climbing or cricket. When I'm not being active, I enjoy gaming (Valorant and Mobile Legends are my go-tos). I'm also a car enthusiast with a soft spot for old JDMs, and a bit of a tech geek who loves discovering new gadgets. You might also find me in the kitchen cooking or baking.

Need an extra player, a gaming buddy, or my Crème Brûlée recipe? Just reach out :)
