Design Atelier Web Landing Page

Web Design Branding B2C

Plura Atelier is a design and build atelier that provides architecture and interior design services such as design, 3D modelling, drawing submission and also renovation works. Plura Atelier may not be big in terms of the size of their design and build atelier, but they have big ideas, thinking approach and creativity.

Being bold is in their DNA. Plura Atelier dare to do what others will not and embrace everything to create impactful work. Moreover, their work quality outcomes is fantastic. In the end, it's just better to have an atelier that will deliver perfect quality.

The Brief

My task was to create a landing page based on Plura Atelier's branding, to market and advertise their completed projects for their target markets.

This landing page was designed with a single call to action: to directly contact their representative for enquiry and request for quotation.

The goal was to introduce the company and services provided, highlighting their completed projects, and to increase future client engagement.

The Process

Brief & Research

After I sit down with the client and was given the brief, I did my research on the brand and collected examples to best highlight the services and completed projects.

Design Stage

I produced two sets of design for the client to choose from. Based on their feedback I did some iterations on the design.

Final Handoffs

I sent my Figma file to the developer and currently under development for the clients to use as one of their marketing materials.
