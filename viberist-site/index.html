Good questions to ask before you start.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Send us a message.

How much does a website cost?

Website design starts from RM350 for a landing page, or RM500 for a 3-page design. Complete websites (design and development) start from RM1,350.

How long does a website take?

A typical website takes around 2–3 weeks from receiving the required content and information. The timeline can vary depending on project scope and how quickly feedback and materials are provided.

How many pages are included?

Standard packages include either 3 or 5 primary pages. Additional simple pages can be added for RM100 each. Project or product detail pages can use reusable templates, with the scope agreed before development.

What do I need to provide?

No technical knowledge is needed. You provide your content (business information, services and website copy), your assets (logo, photos and other brand materials), consolidated feedback during the included revision rounds, and your approval of the design and final website before launch. Timelines depend partly on how quickly these come in.

Do you provide hosting and a domain?

We handle the technical setup and deployment. Your business should own its domain; if you don’t have one yet, we can help you choose and configure it. You pay the actual cost of the domain and any third-party hosting or service fees.

Who owns my website?

You do. Your domain, hosting and website-related accounts remain under your ownership. Viberist manages the technical setup as required.

Can I request changes after the website launches?

Yes. Small website updates are available at RM150 per request. Larger changes or new functionality will be quoted separately.

Do you build ecommerce websites?

Our standard packages focus on professional static websites. Ecommerce, booking systems, CMS platforms, databases and other advanced functionality can be discussed as a separate project.
