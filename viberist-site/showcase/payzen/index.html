A Problem Hiding in Plain Sight

Brodie Rees knew the frustration firsthand. Contractors weren't struggling because they lacked tools. They were struggling because they had too many.

Quotes lived in one app. Invoices lived somewhere else. Payments required another step. The job might be finished, but the admin wasn't.

The solution was clear: create a single workflow that takes a job from quote to payment.

Payzen was built on that idea.

  • Rock-Solid Agreements

    Set clear terms upfront, ensuring smooth transactions and peace of mind for homeowners and tradesmen.

  • Progress-Powered Payments

    Securely release funds in stages as projects advance, protecting both parties.

  • Ultimate Security with Stripe

    Keep your funds safe and visible with our Stripe-integrated payment system.

Finding the Bigger Opportunity

Every founder starts with a vision. The smartest ones stay open to where it leads.

Working closely with Appetiser, Brodie refined Payzen as he learned more about what businesses needed. The app evolved from solving individual contractor challenges to helping organisations manage invoicing and payments at scale.

The result wasn't the app he first imagined. It was the one the market needed.
