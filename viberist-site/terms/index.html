Terms

Last updated 1 October 2026

These terms summarise how Viberist projects work. Each project’s scope, price and timeline are confirmed in writing before work begins, and that agreement takes priority over this page.

Scope and pricing

Prices on this website are starting prices in Malaysian ringgit. Standard packages cover 3 or 5 primary pages. Additional simple pages are RM100 each. Ecommerce, booking systems, CMS platforms and other advanced functionality are quoted separately.

Revisions

Design packages include up to 2 rounds of consolidated feedback. Further revisions or changes to the agreed scope may be quoted separately.

Your content and approvals

You provide the content, images and brand assets for your website and confirm you have the right to use them. Timelines depend partly on how quickly content, feedback and approvals are provided.

Third-party costs

Hosting and domain setup are included with development. Domain registration, hosting usage charges and paid external services are paid by the client.

Ownership

Your domain, website repository and hosting accounts remain under your ownership. Viberist may show completed work in its portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Updates after launch

Small updates are available at RM150 per request. Larger changes and new functionality are quoted separately.
